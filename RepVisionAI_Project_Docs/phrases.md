# RepVision AI — UI Copy and Phrases

Use short, clear language. Keep feedback supportive and avoid shaming the user. Form feedback is an estimate, not medical advice.

## Brand voice
- Encouraging
- Calm and direct
- Practical
- Non-judgmental
- Transparent about uncertainty

## Home screen
- Brand: `REPVISION AI`
- Headline: `Train smarter. Move better.`
- Subtitle: `Your AI-powered workout companion.`
- Section: `Choose your workout`
- Push-ups: `Upper body • Chest, shoulders and triceps`
- Squats: `Lower body • Quads, glutes and hips`

## Setup screen
- Eyebrow: `WORKOUT CONFIGURATION`
- Sets label: `Number of sets`
- Reps label: `Repetitions per set`
- Summary: `Total target: {total} repetitions`
- Primary action: `Continue to camera setup`

## Camera instructions
- Heading: `Set up your camera`
- `Place your phone on a stable stand.`
- `Keep your full body visible throughout the movement.`
- `Use good lighting and avoid cluttered backgrounds.`
- `Move objects that block your body or joints.`
- `Check that the camera angle matches the guidance for this exercise.`
- Permission explanation: `RepVision AI needs camera access to show your movement and analyze body landmarks during your workout.`

## Camera and pose states
- `Checking camera…`
- `Move back until your full body is visible.`
- `Improve the lighting to help body detection.`
- `Your body is not fully visible. Adjust your position.`
- `Pose detected. Hold still for a moment.`
- `Ready when you are.`
- `Pose detection is temporarily unavailable. Reposition and try again.`
- `Camera permission is needed to start a session.`
- `Camera access is off. You can enable it in Android settings.`

## Workout states
- `Get ready`
- `Workout in progress`
- `Set {current} of {target}`
- `Rep {current} of {target}`
- `Set complete`
- `Rest when you need to.`
- `Workout paused`
- `Finish workout`
- `Resume workout`
- `End session`

## Form feedback
Use only when supported by the selected exercise logic and model:
- `Form looks consistent for this repetition.`
- `Your body alignment changed during the movement.`
- `Try to use a controlled range of motion.`
- `Keep your full body in frame so the movement can be assessed.`
- `Not enough visual information to assess this repetition.`
- `Form assessment unavailable — repetition still recorded.`

Do not use these phrases as definitive coaching claims until the corresponding detection logic has been implemented and tested.

## Safety and transparency
- `Form feedback is an estimate and may be inaccurate.`
- `Stop if you feel pain, dizziness, or unusual discomfort.`
- `RepVision AI is not a medical diagnostic tool.`
- `You control whether workout clips are saved.`
